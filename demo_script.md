# 60-second demo script

**0-10s:** "Maria leads an STXBP1 patient community. Today she needs to find related diseases, researchers, and trial assets without spending months in spreadsheets."

**10-22s:** Search `STXBP1` under the Buffalo logo. Check Disease similarity and Mechanism path together. "One search reveals comparison diseases and the shared biology. We can combine views without adding researcher clutter."

**22-34s:** Click Dravet. Show Symptoms, Ask ChatGPT, Publications, and Patient groups. Click a graph edge. "Families get a short summary, reading links ordered by verified journal IF, and patient communities. Each connection has evidence and confidence. Unknown IF stays unknown."

**34-43s:** Open Researchers, then Show more. "Researchers are ranked by papers about the searched disease in our curated dataset. Related experts appear separately, with direct and related topic matches clearly labeled."

**43-53s:** Return to Graph and click Explain in plain language, then Suggested Action. "OpenAI turns a sourced connection into a short explanation when an API key is configured. The next step is concrete: ask an expert about endpoints or compare outcome measures."

**53-60s:** Search `unknown biomarker`, then mention the 10x vision. "Missing evidence is visible. Our 10x target concerns research planning, not a proven acceleration of drug development."

Recording checklist: record the browser at 1440 x 900, avoid exposing credentials, use the English narration above, and export a 60-second MP4. If the API key is absent, describe Explain as a preview and do not claim a live model response.
